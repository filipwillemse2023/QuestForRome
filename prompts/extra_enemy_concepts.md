# description
There still need to be some additions to the standard enemy mechanisms:

- Item drops: When en enemy is killed, there is a chance for an item drop to occur. It should be possible to define enemy drop tables. A drop table is a list of one or more items (from the standard items list, excluding container items). In the list, it should be possible for each of these items to set a weight of probability for that item to effectively drop in a percentage. The sum of all percentages should never exceed 100, but it can be less than 100, in that case the remaining percentage is the chance that no item drops at all. So for example if we make a drop list containing the 1 coin item and the heart item and we set the percentage for the coin to 5 and for the heart to 10, then there is a 5% chance of a coin to drop after the enemy is killed and 10% for a heart to drop in the remaining 85% of the cases, nothing gets dropped. Dropping an item is basically this simple concept: after the death animation of the enemy is finished, the item in that drops (if any) appears at the coordinates that the enemy was at when he died (the center of the enemy hitbox should be the center of the hitbox of the item) where it will remain available for pickup by the player for a duration of 6 seconds (this should be adjustable to any amount of seconds via the global settings). After 4.5 seconds, the item should start blinking and when the 6 seconds are over, the item simply disappears (unless picked up in the meantime of course). Provide the necessary screens in the editor for these drop list definitions (using the standard practices already used for earlier screens)
On each enemy, a drop table needs to be selected from the list of available drop tables.
- Respawning: When a player enters a screen, enemies should always respawn at their start positions (even when the player had visited the screen before and the enemies had moved). Enemies that had been killed, will not respawn however. Unless the player has killed all enemies on a screen, in that case all enemies will respawn the next time the player enters that screen again IF he has traversed at least 6 different other screens in the meantime. When a player exits a map and then reenters that map, all screens will respawn all their enemies even when not all had been killed or no 6 screens had been traversed.
- Vulnerabilities: It should be possible on an enemy definition to indicate that the enemy is not vulnerable to certain weapons or projectiles. You should be able to add weapons and projectiles to an invulnerability list in the edit enemy screen in the editor. And of course in the game, the enemy should no longer take damage from those weapons and projectiles (there will also be no knockback in that case and the enemy will behave like a solid object towards the projectile)
