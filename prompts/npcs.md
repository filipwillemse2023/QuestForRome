# description
It should be possible to define NPC's in the game. These are comparable to enemies (i.e. they have standing and moving animations and hitboxes and we can define a sequence of movement types with associated timings and speeds) but they basically only have two movement types: standing still or random movement, both with minimum and maximum timings and speed for the movement. 

Hitboxes on NPCs basically work like solid hitboxes of tiles, i.e. players and npcs cannot move through each other and stop each other's movement. No damage is ever dealt by an NPC to the player or vice-versa. When pressing the standard action button (normally associated with weapon A) when close to an NPC (i.e. NPC hitbox + 8 pixels margin on all sides), a text will be displayed (this text is defined on the NPC settings). Make sure the editor interface shows all created NPCs like the palettes of enemies or characters (i.e. panels with images, names and the first part of the associated text that can be fit into the panel).
